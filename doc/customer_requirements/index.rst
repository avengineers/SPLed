Customer Requirements
=====================

Customer A - Disco Light
------------------------

.. req:: Disco RQ-1
   :id: REQ_DISCO_1

   The lighting shall automatically sync with any ambient music, adjusting its blink rate
   to match the beats or rhythm.

.. req:: Disco RQ-2
   :id: REQ_DISCO_2

   Users shall have the freedom to choose a specific color for the light. However, in the
   absence of a user-defined color, the software shall delight the user with randomized
   colors, ensuring an authentic disco experience.

.. req:: Disco RQ-3
   :id: REQ_DISCO_3

   As the essence of a disco environment is dynamic and lively, light transitions shall
   happen instantaneously without any lag.

.. req:: Disco RQ-4
   :id: REQ_DISCO_4

   The illumination provided shall be vibrant and noticeable, ranging from medium to high
   levels to keep up with the lively ambiance of disco settings.

Customer B - Sleep Light
------------------------

.. req:: Sleep RQ-1
   :id: REQ_SLEEP_1

   The light emitted shall be constant without any blink, ensuring a non-disruptive
   environment for rest.

.. req:: Sleep RQ-2
   :id: REQ_SLEEP_2

   Color of the light shall be fixed to a warm white, known for its soothing and calming
   properties.

.. req:: Sleep RQ-3
   :id: REQ_SLEEP_3

   The brightness of the light shall be adjustable.

.. req:: Sleep RQ-4
   :id: REQ_SLEEP_4

   The light color shall be blue.

Customer C - Spa Light
----------------------

.. req:: Spa RQ-1
   :id: REQ_SPA_1
   :links: REQ_SPA_4
   :implements: REQ_SPA_2

   The light shall have a slow, rhythmic blink that gives a sensation of calm and peace.

.. req:: Spa RQ-2
   :id: REQ_SPA_2

   Instead of a single color, the lighting shall cycle through multiple predefined colors
   that are commonly associated with relaxation and tranquility.

.. req:: Spa RQ-3
   :id: REQ_SPA_3

   Transitions between colors shall be smooth and seamless, ensuring a continuous flow of
   relaxation.

.. req:: Spa RQ-4
   :id: REQ_SPA_4

   The brightness of the light shall be adjustable.
